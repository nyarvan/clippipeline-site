Clip Pipeline

Clip Pipeline is a small self-hosted tool that turns a video clip into a vertical short with subtitles, shows a preview to its owner for approval, and then publishes it to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok channels.

How it works

  1. The owner sends a link to a Twitch or YouTube clip to a private Telegram bot.
  2. The tool downloads the clip, adds subtitles and generates a title, description and hashtags.
  3. The owner reviews the preview and approves it.
  4. Only after approval, the video is uploaded to the channels the owner connected.

Who it is for

The tool runs on the owner's own computer for personal use and for a small group of people the owner invites. It is not a public service and has no public sign-up. Every description it publishes credits the original author and links to the source clip.
