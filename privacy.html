Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

Clip Pipeline ("the app") is a self-hosted tool operated by its owner on the owner's own computer. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data the app handles

  • Google / YouTube account access. When you connect a YouTube channel, the app (through a self-hosted Postiz instance) receives an OAuth token that lets it upload videos to that channel and read basic channel information. The token is stored only in the database of the owner's own server.
  • TikTok account access. The same applies to a connected TikTok account.
  • Telegram data. The Telegram user ID and chat ID of people allowed to use the bot, and the links, texts and videos they send or approve.
  • Video content. Clips downloaded from the links you send, the rendered videos, subtitles and generated titles and descriptions. Files are deleted automatically after a retention period (48 hours by default).

How the data is used

Data is used only to provide the function you asked for: processing a clip and publishing it to the channels you connected after you approved it. The app does not upload anything without your approval.

Text from a clip (transcript, title and author name) is sent to a language model to generate the title, description and hashtags.

Sharing

The app does not sell data and does not share it with third parties, except with the services needed to do the job: YouTube and TikTok (to publish what you approved), Telegram (to talk to you) and the language model provider (to write the text).

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using YouTube features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

  • Media files are removed automatically after the retention period.
  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and to your TikTok account in the TikTok app settings.
  • To have your stored data (tokens, job history) deleted, write to the contact address below and it will be removed.

Security

Secrets are kept in the owner's server configuration, access to the bot is limited to an allowlist of Telegram users, and the publishing service is reachable only from the owner's computer.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
